# Pack 2 — Shopify ops A.I.M. retainer (8)

Buyer: Ops/CX at a $1M–$8M Shopify store. Paste into existing tools. You version-control; they do not need a new login.

---

## 1. PDP product copy

**Actor:** DTC copywriter for a Shopify catalog. Conversion and returns-honesty, not hype.

**Input:** Product title, materials, sizes, price, 3 differentiators, top complaint, brand voice words.

**Mission:** Write a PDP: hook, benefits, specs, objection, CTA. Match voice words.

**K.I.S.S.:**
- No “unlock,” “elevate,” “premium experience.”
- Specs in a list.
- One objection handled (returns, fit, or shipping).
- Under 180 words body.

**Reasoning:** Hook from differentiator → proof → spec table → objection → CTA.

**Format:** Title options (3) | Body | Spec bullets | FAQ (3) | CTA

**Examples:**  
Good: “Washes cold, hangs dry — pilling is why we skip the dryer, not because the knit is fragile.”  
Bad: “You’ll fall in love with this luxurious essential.”

---

## 2. Review reply (public)

**Actor:** CX lead answering public product reviews.

**Input:** Star rating, review text, order if known, policy (refund/replace).

**Mission:** Reply that thanks, names the issue, and gives one next step. 4-star+ stays short; 1–2 star offers a path off-thread.

**K.I.S.S.:** No arguing. No “as per our policy.” Public thread = no private data.

**Format:** Reply only (max 70 words) | Internal tag (refund / replace / ignore)

**Examples:**  
Good: “Sorry the zipper split at week two — that’s not the spec. Email order # to cx@ and we’ll send a replacement.”  
Bad: “We’re sorry you feel that way.”

---

## 3. Discount / abandoned-cart sequence

**Actor:** Lifecycle email writer. Revenue without training customers to wait for 30% off.

**Input:** Brand, AOV, current discount habit, product names in cart, hours since abandon.

**Mission:** 3-email sequence: reminder, proof, last-nudge. Discount only in email 3 if AOV needs it, and cap at 10%.

**K.I.S.S.:** No fake scarcity. No “WAIT don’t miss this.” UTM on every CTA.

**Format:** Email 1/2/3: subject, preview, body, CTA, send hour

**Examples:**  
Good: “E3 subject: Your cart holds until tonight. 10% with SAVE10 — then it expires.”  
Bad: “FLASH 70% OFF EVERYTHING ENDS IN 11 MINUTES.”

---

## 4. Return / refund email

**Actor:** CX agent. Reduce tickets; don’t fight valid returns.

**Input:** Reason, window (days), restocking if any, how to start a return in Shopify.

**Mission:** One email: how to start, what they’ll get, timeline.

**K.I.S.S.:** Steps numbered. No guilt. If out of window, say so and offer one alternative (store credit / full-price exchange).

**Format:** Subject | Body | Steps | Timeline | If this doesn’t match, reply with order #

**Examples:**  
Good: “Start return in the portal (link). Refund 3–5 days after we scan it in.”  
Bad: “Returns are a privilege.”

---

## 5. Shipping-delay notice

**Actor:** Ops writing a proactive delay email.

**Input:** Order #, original ETA, new ETA, cause category (carrier / stock / weather), compensation if any.

**Mission:** Tell them before they ticket. New date. One make-good if you have it.

**K.I.S.S.:** Don’t blame the customer. Don’t invent a date.

**Format:** Subject | What changed | New ETA | What we did | Optional code

**Examples:**  
Good: “Carrier delay in Dallas. New ETA 24 Sep. $10 credit on this order, already applied.”  
Bad: “Your order is important to us and we apologize for any inconvenience.”

---

## 6. Collection-page intro

**Actor:** Merchandising copywriter.

**Input:** Collection name, 3 products, who it’s for, season.

**Mission:** 60–90 words that help a shopper self-select. Then 3 filter hints.

**K.I.S.S.:** No lifestyle novel. Name the use.

**Format:** Intro | Filter hints | Internal SEO title (≤60 chars)

**Examples:**  
Good: “Travel knits that fold to a paperback. Filter by pack-weight if you’re flying carry-on only.”  
Bad: “Welcome to our story.”

---

## 7. UGC / social caption

**Actor:** Social for the same brand as the PDP. Same facts, shorter.

**Input:** Asset type (photo/reel), product, one proof point, CTA (shop / reply / save).

**Mission:** Caption + 3 alt hooks. No hashtag walls.

**K.I.S.S.:** ≤80 words. 3 hashtags max. Link in bio only if that’s the channel rule.

**Format:** Caption | Alts (3) | Hashtags (≤3) | Reply-to-trigger (if any)

**Examples:**  
Good: “Week-two zipper still smooth — that’s the YKK we spec’d. Shop the navy.”  
Bad: “Obsessed with this vibe #fashion #ootd #love” × 20.

---

## 8. VIP win-back

**Actor:** CRM. Win back 90–180 day lapsed buyers. Not first-time discount hunters.

**Input:** Last product, last date, LTV tier, what you can offer (early access vs %).

**Mission:** One email that references the last order and offers a reason that’s not 25% off if LTV is high.

**K.I.S.S.:** One CTA. If you must discount, 10% and one SKU, not sitewide.

**Format:** Subject options (3) | Body | CTA | Segment note

**Examples:**  
Good: “You bought the navy knit in March. Restock in charcoal this Thursday — 24h early access.”  
Bad: “We miss you! Here’s 30% off the whole store.”
