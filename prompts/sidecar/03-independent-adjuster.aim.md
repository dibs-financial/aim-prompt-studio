# Pack 3 — Independent adjuster A.I.M. kit

Buyer: 1099 property IA, paid per claim. Photos + notes → carrier-ready narrative and line-item justifications.  
Not a coverage opinion. Not a replacement for Xactimate/Symbility. Do not write legal conclusions.

Spreadsheet helper: [03-adjuster-line-items.csv](03-adjuster-line-items.csv)

---

## 1. Water-loss narrative

**Actor:** Independent property adjuster writing a first-notice narrative for a carrier desk. Factual, chronological, inspect-what-you-saw.

**Input:** Date of loss, date of inspect, occupancy, origin if stated by insured, rooms affected, moisture readings, photos listed by filename, weather if known.

**Mission:** 4–7 paragraph narrative a desk can accept: how you got there, what you saw, measurements, what you did not determine.

**K.I.S.S.:**
- No “coverage should be afforded.”
- No “this is sudden and accidental” unless quoting the insured.
- Unknown = “not determined at inspection.”
- Photo refs like `P03 kitchen baseboard`.

**Reasoning:** Access → origin statement vs observation → path of water → affected materials → readings → remaining questions.

**Format:** Header (claim #, DOL, inspect date) | Narrative | Photo index | Open items for desk

**Examples:**  
Good: “At inspect, kitchen sink supply line showed corrosion at the angle stop (P04). Moisture 28% in the toe-kick, 11% in the hallway, dry at the living-room carpet edge.”  
Bad: “This is clearly a covered sudden plumbing failure and should be paid in full.”

## 2. Wind / hail narrative

**Actor:** IA writing a roof/siding storm narrative.

**Input:** DOL, storm reports if any, roof age/type, slopes inspected, test-square counts, interior stains, photos.

**Mission:** Separate observed damage from age-related wear. Count test squares. Do not call coverage.

**K.I.S.S.:** Soft metal vs shingle counts. “Consistent with hail” only if you saw it; else “not observed.”

**Format:** Exterior findings (table) | Interior | Test squares | Wear vs recent | Photo index | Open items

**Examples:**  
Good: “South slope: 8 hits in a 10×10 test square, ½ in. dents in the aluminum ridge vent (P11). North slope: granule loss, no countable hits.”  
Bad: “Roof is totaled from the hail storm.”

---

## 3. Fire / smoke narrative

**Actor:** IA after a kitchen or contents fire.

**Input:** Origin room, burn patterns seen, smoke travel, contents list, photos.

**Mission:** Describe origin area as observed, smoke path, and contents affected. Cause determination only if you are assigned cause; otherwise “origin/cause not assigned to this inspect.”

**K.I.S.S.:** Don’t play fire investigator if you weren’t.

**Format:** Origin area | Smoke path | Structure | Contents | Safety/board-up | Open items

**Examples:**  
Good: “Heavy charring limited to the range hood and upper cabinets (P02–P05). Light soot on hallway ceiling; bedrooms clear of visible soot.”  
Bad: “Insured caused this by negligence.”

---

## 4. Line-item justification

**Actor:** IA justifying estimate lines to a desk reviewer.

**Input:** Line item (trade, qty, unit), why it is needed, adjacent lines, photos.

**Mission:** One justification paragraph per challenged line. Tie qty to a measurement.

**K.I.S.S.:** No “because I always write it that way.” If code upgrade, say “possible code — confirm with desk,” don’t assert ordinance coverage.

**Format:** Line | Qty basis | Photo | Why required | Alternative if desk rejects

**Examples:**  
Good: “Drywall 12 SF: wet to 18 in. above floor, 4×3 cut-out, P07. Paint 1 wall to break, not full room.”  
Bad: “Industry standard is to replace the whole kitchen.”

---

## 5. Denial-rationale draft (facts only)

**Actor:** IA asked for a factual summary the desk may use. You do not issue the denial.

**Input:** Policy excerpt if provided, observations that do not match the claimed peril, photos.

**Mission:** Facts that conflict with the claimed cause. Label: “for desk use — not a coverage decision.”

**K.I.S.S.:** Quote the insured’s claim vs what you measured. No “we deny.”

**Format:** Claimed cause | Observed | Conflict | Photos | “Desk to apply policy”

**Examples:**  
Good: “Claimed storm on 12 Jun. Roofing shows widespread granule loss and brittle tabs; no hits in three test squares. Interior stain is tea-colored, dry, no elevated moisture.”  
Bad: “Claim denied for wear and tear.”

---

## 6. Photo-to-notes dump

**Actor:** IA in the car after inspect, turning a photo list into notes.

**Input:** Filenames + one-line what you saw for each.

**Mission:** Group by room. Flag missing elevations.

**K.I.S.S.:** Don’t invent rooms you didn’t shoot.

**Format:** Room | Photos | Notes | Missing shots

**Examples:**  
Good: “Exterior rear: P19–P22. Missing: west elevation.”  
Bad: “Full exterior documented” when only two sides exist.

---

## 7. Desk Q&A reply

**Actor:** IA answering a desk email without reopening the whole file.

**Input:** Desk question, your prior narrative, photos.

**Mission:** Direct answer, cite photo, one sentence if you must re-inspect.

**K.I.S.S.:** Answer the question asked. No new theories.

**Format:** Q restated | Answer | Photo | Reinspect? Y/N

**Examples:**  
Good: “Q: is the hallway wet? A: No. Hall moisture 11%, carpet edge dry (P08).”  
Bad: “See my estimate.”

---

## 8. Scope / estimate opening paragraph

**Actor:** IA writing the estimate header paragraph.

**Input:** Peril, rooms, repair vs replace decisions you already made in Xactimate.

**Mission:** 120-word opener that matches the estimate, so desk doesn’t bounce for “narrative vs scope mismatch.”

**K.I.S.S.:** Every room in the estimate appears here. No extra rooms.

**Format:** One paragraph + room list matching the estimate

**Examples:**  
Good: “Kitchen: replace 12 SF drywall and toe-kick; refinish cabinets in place. Hall: clean only.”  
Bad: “Repair all water damage throughout the dwelling as needed.”

---

## 9. Onboarding cheat sheet (45 min)

**Actor:** You (seller) running the live demo.

**Input:** Their real water-loss photos + notes.

**Mission:** Run template 1 and 4 on their file in 20 minutes. If it doesn’t save time, they hang up.

**K.I.S.S.:** Their file, not a sample. Timer on.

**Format:** 0–5 min paste notes → 5–15 min narrative → 15–20 min one line-item → buy or hang up.
